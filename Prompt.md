## 💡 Prompting Guide & Examples

To get the best results from **Foxux AI**, here are optimized prompt structures for study, task execution, casual chat, and coding.

### 📚 1. Studying & Learning (工作与学习)
Use structured prompts to get clear explanations, summaries, or step-by-step learning paths.

* **Concept Breakdown:**
  > "Explain [Concept] in simple terms as if I am a beginner. Break down the core principles into bullet points and provide a real-world analogy."
* **Study Plan Generator:**
  > "Act as a tutor. Create a 7-day study roadmap to learn [Topic/Subject]. Include daily practice targets and key takeaways."

---

### 📋 2. Homework & Task Execution (作业与任务准备)
Guide the AI to assist with homework or task execution without just giving flat answers.

* **Step-by-Step Homework Helper:**
  > "Solve the following problem step-by-step and explain the logic behind each step: [Insert Problem]."
* **Text Refinement & Essay Structuring:**
  > "Review the text below for logical flow, grammar, and tone. Suggest improvements and outline a clearer structure: [Insert Text]."

---

### 💬 3. Conversational & Roleplay (聊天与角色)
Set specific personas or guidelines for organic interaction.

* **Interactive Discussion:**
  > "You are an expert in [Topic]. Let's discuss [Subject]. Ask me questions one by one to test my knowledge and discuss deeper concepts."
* **Friendly Assistant Persona:**
  > "Act as a helpful, concise AI companion. Answer my questions directly with minimal fluff and a friendly tone."

---

### 💻 4. Coding & Architecture (编程与开发)
Provide context, constraints, and runtime details for accurate code output.

* **Code Generation (With Constraints):**
  > "Write a [Language/Framework] function to [Task]. Use clean architecture, handle edge cases, and include comments explaining the key logic."
* **Bug Fixing & Optimization:**
  > "Review the following [Language] snippet. Identify memory leaks, syntax errors, or performance bottlenecks, and provide the corrected code: [Insert Code]."
